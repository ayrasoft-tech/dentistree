An approach inspired by nature

Preserving What Nature Created.

Biomimetic dentistry considers how natural teeth are structured and function, guiding thoughtful choices when a tooth needs restoration.

In simple terms

Restore with respect for the original.

“Biomimetic” means taking inspiration from living nature. In dentistry, this approach looks at a tooth's natural structure and how it handles everyday function.

When a tooth is damaged, the goal of restorative care is to address the problem while considering strength, function and appearance. Biomimetic principles can help guide decisions about how much tooth structure to preserve and how to restore what has been affected.

Not every tooth or situation is the same. Your dentist will examine the tooth, discuss what the findings mean and explain suitable options, including their considerations.

The principles in practice

Function and appearance, in conversation.

Advanced bonded restorations use adhesive techniques to join restorative material to tooth structure. A dentist considers the location and condition of the tooth, the forces it experiences, and the patient's needs before recommending a restoration.

Preservation-minded care does not mean that one treatment fits everyone. Some teeth may need a different form of restoration, and every option has its own considerations. An individual consultation is needed to decide what may be suitable.
