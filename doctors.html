Chief Dentist

Dr. Arjun Chellath

BDS, MDS
Prosthodontist | Implantologist | Biomimetic Dentist | Dental Educator

With 12 years of experience in dentistry, Dr. Arjun Chellath brings together clinical expertise, precision, and a passion for excellence.

An MDS-qualified Prosthodontist and Implantologist, he specialises in biomimetic dentistry, advanced bonded restorations, veneers, crowns, bridges, dental implants, and smile designing.

His approach goes beyond simply restoring teeth — it is about preserving what is natural, recreating what is lost, and delivering results that look, feel, and function like the real thing.

His clinical expertise also extends to root canal treatments and retreatments, surgical procedures, cast partial dentures, and complete dentures, making him well-equipped to handle a wide spectrum of restorative and rehabilitative dental needs.

Beyond clinical practice, Dr. Arjun is a passionate dental educator and trainer, conducting hands-on workshops across Kerala. Having trained over 500 dentists as of 2026, he continues to share his knowledge and inspire the next generation of clinicians.

“Dentistry is not just about creating beautiful smiles. It is about restoring confidence, preserving nature, and pursuing excellence in every detail.”

Associate Dentist

Dr. Sneha Suresh

BDS
Special interest in biomimetic dentistry
